Pizza

Description

A pizza recipe is fundamentally a framework for an open faced, baked flatbread that balances three distinct core elements: a yeast risen crust, a seasoned sauce, and a blanket of melted cheese.

Core Ingredients

  1. Dough
    • Flour
    • Yeast
    • Water
    • Salt
    • Olive Oil
    • Sugar or Honey
  2. Sauce
    • Tomatoes
    • Garlic
    • Herbs
    • Black Pepper
  3. Cheese and Toppings
    • Cheese
    • Garnish
    • Optional Toppings: Pepperoni, Italian sausage, sliced mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, or olives
