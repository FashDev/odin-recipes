Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked dish made of stacked, wide, flat pasta sheets layered with savory fillings typically a rich meat sauce (ragù), creamy béchamel or ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella then baked until golden and bubbling.

Core Ingredients

  1. Lasagna sheets: Ground beef or pork simmered with
  2. Meat Sauce (Ragù): Ground beef or pork simmered with tomatoes, onions, garlic, and Italian herbs.
  3. Cheese : Ricotta (or a cream-based béchamel sauce), shredded mozzarella for melting, and grated Parmesan.
  4. Aromatics & Seasonings: Salt, black pepper, and fresh or dried herbs like basil and oregano.
