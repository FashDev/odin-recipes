Tacos

Description

Tacos are a traditional Mexican dish consisting of a small, hand sized corn or wheat tortilla topped with a flavorful filling, folded and eaten by hand.

Core Ingredients

  • Tortillas Small corn or flour tortillas (warmed or lightly toasted).
  • Protein / Main Filling: Seasoned ground beef, shredded chicken, pork (like carnitas or al pastor), carne asada, or beans/veggies.
  • Toppings & Aromatics: Chopped fresh cilantro, diced onions, shredded lettuce, and crumbled or shredded cheese (such as cotija or Monterey Jack).
  • Salsas & Acids: Fresh lime wedges for squeezing, along with green (verde) or red (roja) salsa, pico de gallo, or sour cream/crema.
